ShopLike Vintage data transfer

This one-time tool copies your existing store records from Supabase into Firebase. It does not delete or modify anything in Supabase.

Before starting
Sign in using the Firebase administrator account shoplikevintage@gmail.com. Keep this page open until the transfer finishes.
